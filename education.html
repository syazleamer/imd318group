What is Bundle/Thrift/Preloved?

A bundle is a collection of items sold together, usually at a discounted price. Thrift refers to buying second-hand items to save money and reduce waste. Preloved describes second-hand items that were previously owned and cherished.

Tips for Thrifting

Keep an eye out for brand names, consider upcycling or repurposing items, haggle if appropriate, check return policies, and follow thrift stores on social media for updates on new arrivals or special sales.

Sustainability Benefits

Thrifting helps reduce waste, lowers the demand for fast fashion, and supports the environment by minimizing the carbon footprint of clothing production.

Caring for Preloved Items

Ensure your preloved items last long by washing them with care, storing them properly, and mending small damages when needed.

Key Tips for Creating Effective Bundles

Successful bundles include items that complement each other, offer meaningful discounts, and cater to the target audience's needs.
