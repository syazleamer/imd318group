How We Sort Our Items

Your trust is our priority. Learn how we categorize items before they reach your hands!

New or Like New

Items are almost brand new with no visible signs of wear. Perfect for gifting or personal use.

Gently Used

Minimal signs of wear, such as light creasing or small stains. Quality items ready for reuse.

Vintage/Pre-loved

Unique items with visible wear that add to their character and charm.

Damaged

Visible damage but suitable for upcycling or crafting. Find creative uses for these gems!

Repairable

Needs minor fixes like missing buttons or small stitching. Ideal for DIY enthusiasts.
